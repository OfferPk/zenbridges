## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
Immerse yourself in ZenBridges, a tranquil and sleek offline Hashiwokakero (Bridges) island connection logic puzzle designed to soothe your mind and sharpen your deductive reasoning.

Step into a serene nocturnal water garden. Your objective is pure and timeless: connect tranquil stone islands across calm waters using single and double radiant bridges so that each island connects to its exact required bridge count, forming a single continuous, harmonious island network.

With no timers, no pressure, no ads, and zero network requirements, ZenBridges provides a peaceful, mindful sanctuary where classic logic puzzles meet modern aesthetic beauty.

Experience pure procedural sound synthesis driven by the Web Audio API. Each bridge connection rings with meditative water droplet tones and resonant singing stone harmonics, culminating in a celestial pentatonic chord cascade when the network reaches harmony.

• 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST:
Play anywhere, anytime — on a flight, on your commute, or in quiet solitude off the grid. ZenBridges requires no internet connection, no account login, and collects zero personal data.

• INTUITIVE ONE-TAP & DRAG CONTROLS:
Simply tap two islands in line of sight or drag across the water to cycle bridges (0 → 1 → 2 → 0). Responsive 60fps canvas animations make every connection smooth and tactile.

• DEDUCTIVE HINT ASSISTANT & UNLIMITED UNDO:
Never feel stuck. An intelligent constraint-propagation hint engine detects over-capacity islands, points out forced connections, and guides your next move. Experiment freely with unlimited undo and instant reset.

Designed with a deep dark palette, soothing ambient ripples, glowing cyan luminescent bridges, and dynamic island satisfaction indicators (amber under, emerald satisfied, crimson over-capacity).
